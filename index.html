⚔️ Kingdom of WarPoly ⚔️

Welcome, Commander! Build your low-poly realm, expand your economy, train an army, and protect the realm from bandit raiders!


  • 🪓 Chop Trees: Select a Villager and Right-Click on any tree to harvest wood. When full, they automatically deliver wood to the Castle or Lumber Camp!
  • ⛏️ Mine Gold & Stone: Select a Villager and Right-Click the Gold Mine or Stone Quarry to extract precious minerals.
  • 🏠 Construct Buildings: Select a Villager to open the Build Menu (House, Lumber Camp, Barracks, Watchtower, Farm).
  • 🛡️ Train Warriors: Select your Barracks to train heavily armored Knights and sharpshooting Archers.
  • 🏹 Defense & Victory: Bandits raid from the southern outpost! Build Watchtowers to defend, or march your army across the waters to destroy the Bandit Camp for VICTORY!
